✕ dismiss This is the front end of a Kafka + Spark streaming pipeline, running its simulator directly in your browser. Twelve synthetic patients stream vital signs once per second. Each reading is scored with the Modified Early Warning Score (MEWS): 0–3 points per parameter, and a total of 5 or more means immediate clinical review. Some of these patients are quietly deteriorating (sepsis, respiratory failure, cardiac instability); watch the wall long enough and you will see tiles turn amber, then red. Click any patient for their rhythm strip and score history. The production pipeline does exactly this at scale: simulator → Kafka → stream processor → TimescaleDB → API → dashboard. The trace is a synthesized ECG lead sweeping at the patient's current heart rate, refreshed the way a real bedside monitor refreshes: a moving erase bar, not a scrolling window.

The five-spoke glyph is the patient's MEWS rose: one spoke per scored parameter (HR, systolic BP, RR, temperature, consciousness), one filled notch per point. A bare center is a well patient; a full bloom is a MEWS of 15.
